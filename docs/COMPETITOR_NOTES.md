# Internal competitor notes — 2026-08-20

Public Australian roofing sites were analysed only for abstract information architecture. No code, text, imagery, logo, claims or layout was copied.

| Reference | Abstract lesson adopted | Rejected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| https://www.melbournemasterworks.com.au/ | State three core services and make the first conversation concrete. | Their project imagery, wording, guarantees and form. |
| https://www.roofready.com.au/ | Pair service clarity with a simple homeowner-first contact path. | Their metrics, family-owned claim, copy and layout. |
| https://www.trueroof.com.au/ | Explain work categories and make a direct contact option visible. | Their accreditations, commercial offers, booking path and copy. |
| https://roofguardroofing.com.au/ | Use proof/reassurance as a distinct page function. | Their reviews, warranty claims, project photos and presentation. |
| https://roofrestorersmelbourne.com.au/ | Keep the main service set understandable to a homeowner. | Their business claims, copy and visual assets. |

Original concept direction: muted paper, charcoal, clay and eucalyptus palette; a single original hero image; service cards as editorial text rows rather than competitor layouts; phone/mailto conversion only.
