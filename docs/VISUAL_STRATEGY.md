# Internal visual strategy — third direction

## Objective

Make the page read as a friendly, capable Melbourne roof-trade first call: who ProGuard is, what it handles, one independently verifiable confidence signal and a direct phone route in the first screen. It must not resemble a luxury architecture editorial or a generic agency template.

## Reference study — 2026-08-20

Read-only review of five current Australian roofing/trade sites:

1. [Melbourne Masterworks](https://www.melbournemasterworks.com.au/) — compact service grouping and direct contact route.
2. [Roof Ready](https://www.roofready.com.au/) — homeowner-language problem framing and local-trade tone.
3. [True Roof](https://www.trueroof.com.au/) — immediate phone/email routes and concrete service taxonomy.
4. [Roof Guard Roofing](https://roofguardroofing.com.au/) — recognisable roof-service hierarchy and coverage framing.
5. [Roof Restorers Melbourne](https://roofrestorersmelbourne.com.au/) — clear service categories with a local-business voice.

The demo takes only the lessons of composition, density, trust expression and mobile action visibility. It copies no copy, claim, image, layout or identity.

## Selected system

Airy off-white, eucalyptus green, blue-grey and a small clay call colour. The first screen is a compact two-column trade introduction, not a full-bleed editorial billboard. The rest of the page follows a homeowner-observable path:

1. What have you noticed? — tiles, ridge/valley or water path.
2. See the work — six official gallery images express rebedding/repointing and restoration across named Melbourne suburbs.
3. Name the roof detail — bounded repair and gutter-edge scope with no result promises.
4. Call or email — direct business routes only.

## Visual high points

1. Official rebedding/repointing gallery image in the hero beside the independently verifiable review count.
2. Six-item official gallery that makes the actual service mix visible instead of using concept imagery or abstract diagrams.
3. Separate official rebedding/repointing and fascia/downpipe images paired with the narrow repair and water-flow service descriptions.

All displayed photographs are public images from the ProGuard official gallery and are individually recorded in the internal materials ledger. They retain only the official gallery's service/location labels; the demo adds no outcome, timeframe, credential or before/after claim.

## Anti-template test

Replacing the business name fails the test because the story is organised around the exact verified ProGuard scope — restoration, ridge/valley/tile repair, gutters/fascias/downpipes and gutter guard — and contains the business's own official gallery labels for Heidelberg, Thornbury, Eltham, Pascoe Vale, Hawthorn and Templestowe. A generic cleaner, builder or another roofer cannot retain those visual/service mappings and contact evidence unchanged.
